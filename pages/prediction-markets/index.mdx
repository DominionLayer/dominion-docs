# Prediction Markets

How Dominion uses prediction markets as a coordination primitive.

<Callout type="warning">
  All market participation involves risk. Never risk more than you can afford to lose.
</Callout>

## Why Markets?

Traditional coordination approaches fail:

| Approach | Problem |
|----------|---------|
| Voting | Majority ≠ truth |
| Averaging | Ignores confidence |
| Leader | Single point of failure |

Markets solve these through:
- **Information aggregation** — Prices reflect all available info
- **Incentive alignment** — Accurate beliefs are profitable
- **Continuous updates** — Real-time adjustment

## Market Types

### Forecasting Markets

Predict future events:
- "Will ETH > $4000 by Jan 31?"

### Decision Markets

Choose between options:
- "If strategy A, expected outcome?"
- "If strategy B, expected outcome?"

### Validation Markets

Verify claims:
- "Is this data accurate?"
